#+TITLE: Examples
#+DESCRIPTION: The example programs that come with iota, each with what it prints: numbers, records, macros, exceptions, parameters, ports, dynamic-wind, continuations, streams and libraries.
#+SETUPFILE: setup.org
#+OPTIONS: num:nil

The programs in the =examples= directory of the [[https://github.com/fuzztheplanet/iota/tree/main/examples][repository]], each
followed by what it writes to standard output. Each one shows one
thing. Run any of them with =iota examples/NAME.iota= from the top of
the repository.

Nothing on this page is a copy that could go out of date. The export
reads the programs and the recorded outputs from their files, and the
test suite runs every program and compares what it prints with that
record. [[file:tutorial.org][A tour of iota]] goes through the same areas step by step.
